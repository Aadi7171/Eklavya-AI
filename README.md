# 🏹 Eklavya-AI

> **Empowering Rural Bharat with AI-Driven Career Growth and Blockchain-Verified Skills.**

Eklavya-AI is a next-generation educational and career empowerment platform specifically designed for rural Indian communities. By combining **Voice-AI** for seamless onboarding, **Agentic Negotiation** for fair employment, and **Blockchain-Verified Credentials (Poly-badges)**, we aim to bridge the gap between local talent and global opportunities.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🎙️ Voice-Powered Onboarding
Traditional forms can be a barrier. Eklavya allowing users to describe their skills and local projects (e.g., "I manage the village irrigation system" or "I repair tractors") via **voice in regional languages** (Hindi supported). Our AI extracts relevant skills and automatically builds a professional profile.

### 👔 Agentic Negotiator
Empowering users during the hiring process. The platform features an AI-driven "Negotiator" that helps users advocate for better salary and terms by highlighting their verified skills and "Poly-badges," ensuring they are compensated fairly for their expertise.

### ⛓️ Blockchain-Verified Credentials (Poly-badges)
Trust is built through transparency. We issue **Poly-badges** (NFT-based skill credentials) on the **Polygon Blockchain**. Whether it's MS Excel proficiency or technical mechanical skills, these tamper-proof badges provide verifiable proof of expertise.

### 🚜 Rural-First Design
Tailored UI/UX with regional greetings ("Namaste Eklavya") and a focus on local career advancement, ensuring accessibility for users who might be new to digital platforms.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: [React 19](https://react.dev/) + [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Blockchain**: [Polygon](https://polygon.technology/) (for verified skill credentials)
- **Styling**: Modern CSS with a focus on accessibility and responsiveness.
- **AI Integration**: Simulated Voice Processing & Agentic Logic.
